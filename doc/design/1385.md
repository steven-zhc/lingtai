## Where the team section's facts go (the decision the ticket leaves open)

The ticket sends recipe keys to `plugins/`, but `mode`, `branch`, `take` and `pr:` are not plugins. `plugins/index.md:3` says *"a step's behaviour is the plugins the recipe declares there"*, and its table is read off `PLUGINS`. `plugins/` is also out of scope. So each sentence of `:200-267` goes like this:

- **Case (a), linked rather than kept:**
  - The mode table (`:206-211`) and what a team ticket's life is go to `team.md`, chapter 2. It does not exist yet, so `resolveHref` sends it to GitHub, which the ticket expects.
  - `take` refusals, `branch.personal` being required or refused, and the old spellings plus `doctor --fix` (`:236-247`, `:263-267`) are already in `upgrading-to-0.16.md:16,58,142-165`.
  - `pr.ci.required` and `provider: none` (`:254-257`) are already in `github.md:280-283`.
- **Case (b), moved to `reference.md` policy**: the numbers nobody else has. These are `pr.limits` defaults (`turns` 20, `wall` `5m`, from `packages/recipe/src/recipe.ts:3016`), *no `usd`, so it counts against the day's ceiling*, `pr.ci.timeout` default `60m`, an empty `required` being refused, and `jenkins` being refused in 0.16 with its credentials in `config.yml` (`recipe.ts:3019-3039`, `resolve.ts:546-550`). `reference.md:286` describes that section as *"every number that decides behaviour"*, and `:2-4` says each table names where its list lives in code. So cite `recipe.ts`/`resolve.ts` lines there, as the triage subsection does. Before writing a number, check it against the schema; do not copy it from the old prose.
- **Case (c), dropped**: *"so a project made before 0.16 does what it did"*, the `branch.personal` name-validity detail, and the `both` history.
- **What is left on the page**: the `mode` row of *What a choice becomes* stays, as one row and not a table of effects. Under the table goes the ticket's one sentence: `mode` is `solo` or `team`, a recipe that names none is solo, and [team.md] says what team changes.
