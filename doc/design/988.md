## One line in the diagram needs a smaller size, and a Mac will not show why

`doc/img/concepts.svg:39` is the only line at risk. It starts at `x=460` inside a box whose right edge is 846 (`:36`, `446 + 400`).

The font stack at `:7` is `ui-sans-serif,system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",sans-serif`, so its width depends on the reader's machine. I measured the new text at the 12px that `.note` sets (`:8`), with the system font files:

| Font | Width | Line ends at | Box edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| San Francisco | 380 | 840 | 846 |
| Helvetica or Arial | 409 | 869 | 846 |

The old *pass* text already ended at 854 in Helvetica or Arial. On a Mac the new line looks as if it fits, with 6px to spare, so the browser check in *Done when* passes there and says nothing about a machine that falls back to an Arial-width font.

**Decision:** set that one line to 11px without waiting to see an overflow. It then ends at about 835 in Arial and about 808 in San Francisco. This is the "size" change the ticket allows.

Write it as `style="font-size:11px"` on that `<text>`. A `font-size="11"` attribute does nothing, because the `.note` rule in the stylesheet overrides a presentation attribute.

The other changed lines need nothing:
- `:35` ends by 366 at the widest, against a box edge of 418.
- `:28` ends by 368 and sits in no box, clear of the fix-round arrows, which stop at `y=150`.
- `:13` and `:26` are short and sit in no box.
