## Design: #1381, chapter 4 as one attempt

### Three tests the cut can break, and how to keep them green

The ticket mentions none of these. All three are in `apps/site/unit/docs.test.ts`, which `pnpm test` runs.

1. **Six board labels must stay bold** (`docs.test.ts:295-303`, *"quotes only the labels the board shows"*). It counts the `**…**` labels in `concepts.md` that appear in `RESOLUTION_LABELS` (`:110-117`) and asserts `toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(6)`. Today they reach six only because *What you can do at a wait* (`concepts.md:84-100`) lists them all. If you cut that section, the *Two ways to go back* table still has four (`:43-44`). Put the other two back in sections that stay:
   - **Answer the question**: in the one sentence that says why a card stops (see "The structure" below).
   - **Close the ticket**: in *How a ticket ends*, e.g. *"**Closed.** A person chose **Close the ticket** at a wait."*

   Do not lower the threshold. The test exists so the chapter uses the board's own words, and chapters 6–10 copy that.
2. **The steps must appear in order** (`:279-292`). It walks `STEPS` from `@lingtai/domain` and requires each `` `step` `` to appear after the one before. The ten-step table satisfies it. Keep every step name in backticks in that table.
3. **Every anchor in `doc/` must land on a heading** (`:366-405`). Four links point at `#a-prs-life` today, not one:
   - `doc/recipe.md:211`
   - `doc/github.md:273`
   - `doc/upgrading-to-0.16.md:161`
   - `doc/concepts.md:107`, the in-page `[A PR's life](#a-prs-life)` inside *How a ticket ends*

   Point all three outside links at `team.md`, with no anchor. In the chapter, the team-mode sentence in *How a ticket ends* links `team.md` too. `team.md` is not published, so `resolveHref` sends it to GitHub (`docs.ts:498`), and the anchor test skips any link that does not resolve to `/docs/` (`:390`). The tutorial already links the unwritten `team.md` the same way, and its test pins that (`docs.test.ts:135`, `expect(body).toContain('(team.md)')`).

### The one judgement: "why a card stops where it does"

The ticket's **Want** keeps this. Its **What stays** sends *When a step stops* to chapter 7. Those two do not conflict, because they answer different questions:
- Chapter 7 covers how a step stops: refused, did not finish, asked.
- Chapter 4 says where a card stops: at `proposed`, the only step that decides, when the recipe names a person there or when a limit or a question needs one.

Write that as two or three sentences at the end of *One ticket, start to finish*, using **Answer the question**, and link `when-it-fails.md` for the rest.

`when-it-fails.md` does **not** list the three ways a step stops or the six wait moves. It has the failure table and *"Waiting on you … with the moves you can take"* (`when-it-fails.md:43`). So "already covers them" is true of the topic, not of every sentence:
- The three ways a step stops are case (c) or chapter 7's to write. Drop them here, and do not move them into `when-it-fails.md`: that would be editing chapter 7, which is out of scope.
- The six moves stay here as labels in the kept sections (test 1).

### Why *The words* goes

A rough budget, prose with fences removed:

| Part | Words |
|---|---|
| Intro | ~40 |
| Start to finish, with its table | ~200 |
| Two ways to go back, with its table | ~230 |
| How a ticket ends | ~90 |
| Next | ~10 |
| **Total** | **about 570–600** |

The words table is about 330 words, and none of its rows fits on one line. That fails the ticket's own condition, so drop it. Each term it defines (attempt, fix round, restart, wait, landing) is already bold where the kept text first uses it. `glossary.md` covers the rest, but do not add a *Going further* link to it: only one *Next* link is allowed.

If the count lands under 600, put the words back in *How a ticket ends* or the "why it stops" sentences. Do not pad the tables. Check the count with the same arithmetic the tests use, `proseOf` (`docs.test.ts:121-125`): remove the fences, split on whitespace.

### Pin it

This chapter is the model for chapters 6–10, so add a `describe` to `docs.test.ts` like the tutorial's (`:127-148`). It should assert:
- `proseOf(body)` is ≥ 600 and ≤ 800;
- the `##` headings, in order, are `One ticket, start to finish`, `Two ways to go back`, `How a ticket ends`, `Next`;
- the body contains `(board.md)` and does not contain `#a-prs-life`.

That makes each **Done when** box a unit assertion. Nothing here needs `pnpm test:integration`.

Leave these alone:
- `BOOK` and `PUBLIC_SLUGS` (`docs.ts:132-138`, `page.test.tsx:130-143`): `board.md` is not written yet, so it gets no row. The neighbour test (`docs.test.ts:506-510`) still expects the site's own "next" after `concepts` to be `when-it-fails.md`. The page's *Next* link and the site's chapter navigation are two different things.
- `chrome.tsx:26`'s note, *"The steps of an attempt"*: it is still true.

No ADR is touched. 0112 §13 (`doc/README.md:100`) sets up the book. The 600–800 rule is #1376's.
