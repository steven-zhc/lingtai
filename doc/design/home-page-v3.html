The first question

Why not just give the agent the issue?

You can. At the end you get its word for it. With Lingtai, you get the receipt you just watched.

Common questions

Will it break main?

Nothing lands until your checks and the review pass. A check already red on main stops it as base is red — it doesn't spend rounds on breakage that isn't its own.

Will it run up a bill?

Every paid call is recorded. A daily ceiling pauses it until midnight, or until you resume it.

Will it loop forever?

Fix rounds are counted and bounded. When they run out, it stops and asks you — with a recommended answer.

What if my laptop restarts at 3am?

The ticket isn't lost: its commits are kept on a branch, and the next run takes it again from there.

Can it weaken its own checks?

No. The recipe that sets them lives outside the repository the agent edits.

Can it read my ~/.ssh?HONEST ANSWER

By default, yes — a Claude Code agent runs as you. Lingtai keeps its own credentials out of the agent's environment, but it is not a container. Your own sandbox closes what you choose. What an agent can touch →
