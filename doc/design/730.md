## Shape

One change to how the `pass:` line is printed at `packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:4066`. Nothing is added to the walk, to `StepReached`, to `StepEnding` or to any event.

**`skipped` is a word the line prints, not an ending.** Do not add it to the `StepEnding` union (`packages/conductor/src/pass.ts:508`). That union reaches `endingOf`, `outcomeOf` and the router, and the ticket's Out of scope says no new field records "nothing configured". The visit keeps ending `passed`, and only its text on the line changes.

**Pull the line out into a small exported pure function and unit-test it directly.** Call it, say, `passLine(steps: readonly StepReached[], declared: Recipe['steps']): string`, and keep it in `conduct.ts` next to where it is used. The whole-pass fixture cannot cheaply reach two of the Done-when cases:

- `end` with an effect declared;
- a `held` or `refused` visit with empty `results` and an empty recipe list.

A direct test with hand-built `StepReached` values covers both, plus `prepared` with and without an install. The call at `:4066` becomes `log(passLine(pass.steps, recipe.steps))`. `recipe` is in scope there: it is bound at `conduct.ts:680` (`const recipe = resolved.recipe`).

**The rule:**

```
v.ending.ending === 'passed' && v.results.length === 0 && (declared[v.step] ?? []).length === 0
```

When it holds, print `${v.step}=skipped`; otherwise print as today. Read `declared` from the resolved `recipe.steps`, never from the actions the conductor injects on top of it. Two cases show why this is safe:

- **`--no-merge`.** The test calls it "a `human:` action injected after `merge`'s declared list" (`conduct-a-whole-pass.test.ts:57-59`). That visit has non-empty `results` and ends `held`, so it is untouched either way.
- **A routed `proposed`.** It is built with `results: []` (`pass.ts:1330-1333`), but its ending is `routed` (`pass.ts:491`), so the first clause already excludes it.

## Decided here

- **A missing key** (`declared[step] === undefined`) counts as empty. A `steps: {}` recipe is the test fixture, and whether the resolver fills in all ten keys does not change this rule.
- **`end` with effects declared but none matching this outcome** prints `end=passed`. `results` is always `[]` at `end` (`pass.ts:554`: "`results` is `[]` at `end`, whose list is effects and never reaches a pipeline"). So at `end` the recipe-list clause is the only thing that tells the two apart, which is the ticket's Watch out. The direct test must pin it.
- **A step visited twice** goes through the same function per visit. No dedupe, and nothing keyed by step name.
