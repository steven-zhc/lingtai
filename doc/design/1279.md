# #1279: design note. An approval carries across a clean base merge; a conflict after it is reviewed only in its resolution

## Where it goes

Nearly all of it goes in `packages/conductor/src/run-attempt.ts`, in the `MergeBaseBranch` case. The line numbers in the ticket are out of date. The case now starts at `:2073`, the rule's comment is at `:2093-2097`, and the carry is at `:2098-2113`. Its comment says: *"A person's approval of the head the merge started from keeps the earlier read; any other base move on the branch's files is read against the merge baseline (#1274)."* Two smaller changes go in `applyConflictMerge` (`:1671-1701`) and in the 0108 ADR. `packages/actions` is not touched; the reason is under *The overlap waiver* below.

## Today's code, read

- **`approvedAt`** (`:1601-1608`) is set only on a continuation from `proposed` or `merge` that is not a grant and not a clipped read. `approvedHeadStands` is computed at `:2020`, before `record` moves `onSha`.
- **The carry exists only for a disjoint move.** `if (disjoint && (reviewedHeadStands || approvedHeadStands))` at `:2101` sets `reviewCarriedTo = merged.head`. The loop then skips `review` at `:2239`. `mergedSinceReview` is kept only when `reviewedHeadStands` (`:2105`). An approval-only carry therefore keeps no baseline at all.
- **Any other move by an approval:**
  - `mergedSinceReview` is nulled, because `!approvedHeadStands` at `:2120`.
  - `reviewedSha = approvedAt` at `:2130`.
  - `approvedAt = null` at `:2136`.
  - The walk goes back to `build`.
  - The review that follows gets a plain `reviewedSha...HEAD` delta. The comment at `:2096-2097` itself says that is wrong after a merge: *"after a merge `reviewedSha...HEAD` is the base's commits"*. That is how #1258's reviewer re-raised everything.
- **Conflict path.** `applyConflictMerge` takes its baseline from `reviewedSha ?? mergedSinceReview?.reviewed` (`:1682`). Neither is set in an approval continuation, because `reviewedSha` starts `null` at `:1576` and only a review visit sets it (`:2492`). So a conflict after an approval gets a full review.
- **How the review decides what blocks.** The blocking set is the delta's hunks plus `merged.overlap`, from `agent-action.ts:1199`: `merged !== undefined && context.reviewedSha === undefined ? new Set(merged.overlap) : undefined`. The prompt names the overlap files as open for new findings only when the list is non-empty (`agent-action.ts:495-499`).

## The shape

### 1. One branch for an approval, ahead of the disjoint test

In the `MergeBaseBranch` case, put `if (approvedHeadStands)` first. It covers every clean merge, whatever the `BaseMoved` paths are: disjoint, overlapping, or `null`. A `null` list is safe to carry here because this route is only taken on a merge the lane proved clean (`:2075-2076`, *"a tree the lane just proved clean"*). The branch does five things:

- **`reviewCarriedTo = merged.head`**, so `review` is skipped at `:2239`, as #1210's carry is.
- **`mergedSinceReview = { reviewed: <approved head>, base: next.toSha, overlap: [] }`.** On the first carry, the approved head is `approvedAt`. On a later carry it is the previous `mergedSinceReview.reviewed`, so the baseline stays the head the person actually read. This value is never shown to a reviewer while the carry stands. It is there for the review that a red build's fix round will buy (see *Red build* below).
- **`reviewedSha = null`.** Never the approved head, because that is the plain delta the `:2096` comment calls wrong.
- **`landedOnBase = null`**, as the disjoint carry does at `:2113`. With nothing on the overlap paths left to block, the *base moved on these paths* section would only invite findings that end up `minor`.
- **`approvedAt = merged.head`, not `null`.** The approval follows the system's own merges and nothing else. A second base move arrives with `onSha === approvedAt` and is carried again, with the baseline unchanged; that is the ticket's *second base move* edge. Any agent commit moves `onSha` away and the approval no longer stands, as the edge asks.

This is how I read the Watch out's *"one merge deep per approval"*: the head that lands is the approved head plus base merges that `build` passed, and never an agent's change made after the approval. `baseBranchMerges` still bounds how many merges there are.

### 2. The existing disjoint/overlap branches

These keep their current behaviour for the `reviewedHeadStands` case. Remove `approvedHeadStands` from their conditions (`:2101`, `:2120`) and drop `reviewedSha = approvedHeadStands ? approvedAt : null` (`:2130`); an approval can no longer reach those lines. Keep `approvedAt = null` at `:2136`, but only on the paths that are not approvals.

### 3. Conflict after an approval

At the `FixRefusal` call (`:2204`), pass `approvedHeadStands ? approvedAt : null` into `applyConflictMerge`. Inside it, the baseline becomes `reviewedSha ?? mergedSinceReview?.reviewed ?? approved`, and the overlap is `[]` whenever the baseline came from an approval. The overlap stays `[]` even if a previous carry already set `mergedSinceReview`. Everything else stays as it is: `landedOnBase` with `conflicted`, the lost-changes question, and the conflict round's own counter. The review after the round then reads `merge-tree(approved, base)` against `HEAD`, which is the resolution, through the existing `agent-action.ts:1031` path. Anything outside those hunks is demoted to `minor`.

### 4. The overlap waiver lives in run-attempt.ts

Writing `overlap: []` is how *"the overlap rule does not apply after an approval"* gets built. Nothing in `ActionContext` (`action.ts:645`) changes. To keep the overlap empty, carry a local flag next to `mergedSinceReview`, something like `baselineApproved: boolean`. The union at `:2127` and at `:1689` must not add paths while the flag stands. Clear the flag wherever `mergedSinceReview` is cleared by a whole read (`:2493-2494`). From then on a review has read a later head, and #1274's ordinary rule applies again.

## Red build

A red `build` after the carry is a `FixRefusal`. That path clears `reviewCarriedTo` (`:2196`) and the fixer's commit moves `onSha`, so the approval no longer stands. The fix round's review then runs with `mergedSinceReview` from §1 still in place. It reads the fix against `merge-tree(approved head, base)`, with no overlap, so a finding the person waived lands outside the delta and becomes `minor`.

This is deliberate. Without §1's `mergedSinceReview`, the fix round would get the full review that the existing test at `a-base-that-moved.test.ts:316` (*"reviews the next head in full after a red build and a fix round"*) pins for the non-approval case. That would put #1258 back one round later.

## Restart halfway

The ticket says *"the carry is read from the walk like #1210's"*. Nothing in the code backs that. `reviewCarriedTo` is in memory only (`:1592`, *"Never shared between passes"*), and `attempts.ts` has no reader for an approval or a carry. `approvedAt` is recomputed only for a continuation from `proposed` or `merge` (`:1605`).

So a daemon that dies between the carry and the landing comes back as a continuation that does not know about the approval. It is reviewed by #1274's existing continuation path, with `reviewedHeadOf` (`attempts.ts:711`) and `landedOnBase`, and the overlap is open again. That is today's behaviour, not something unsafe. Leave it, write it in 0108 as the known gap, and do not add an event for it in this ticket.

The same goes for the resumed-conflict path at `:2374`, which uses `options.from.reviewedHead` with the overlap open. If anyone needs to confirm it later, the fields to read are:
- the `MergeBaseBranch` route's `toSha`, in the run's `RouteTaken` events;
- whether a `StepStarted { step: 'review' }` follows it before the next `merge`.

## Other edges

- **Two passes at once:** every new variable is per-attempt, local to `runAttempt`.
- **Red build:** `REFUSING_STEPS` still applies, so a red build is never waived.
- **`approvedAt` null** (a grant, a clipped read, or a pass with no approval): §1 is never entered and the code paths are unchanged.

## Tests

Put them in `packages/conductor/unit/a-base-that-moved.test.ts` and use its `walk(..., { step: 'proposed', waive: [] })` harness:

- **Change one existing test.** `:271`, *"names the approved head to the review of the merge that came after the approval"*, flips. Its assertion `reviewSaw[0]?.reviewedSha === 'abc1234def'` is exactly the behaviour being removed. It becomes: `reviewSaw` is empty, the steps after the merge are `merge, build, proposed, merge`, and the walk ends `Landed`.
- **Clean merge after an approval.** Use `moved('base-1')` (an overlap) and `moved('base-1', null)`: no review, `Landed`.
- **Red build after the carry.** A fix round is bought, and that round's review sees `mergedSinceReview: { reviewed: 'abc1234def', base: 'base-1', overlap: [] }` and no `reviewedSha`.
- **Two moves in a row.** Both are carried, and the baseline is still `abc1234def`.
- **Conflict after an approval.** Put this one in `a-conflict-round-starts-merged.test.ts`. The review after the round sees `mergedSinceReview.reviewed === <approved head>` and `overlap: []`, with `landedOnBase.conflicted` still set.
- **Demotion.** The *"a new major outside the delta is `minor`"* item is a property of `reviewAnswer` once `overlap` is empty. Pin it at the context level as above. If you also want it at the action level, add a case in `packages/actions/unit/agent-action.test.ts` with `mergedSinceReview.overlap: []` and a finding on a path that is not in the delta.

Everything above is unit-level. Nothing here needs `pnpm test:integration`.

## 0108

Edit item 13's `mergeBaseBranch` paragraph (`doc/decisions/0108-a-refusal-buys-a-round.md:355-364`) in place. 0108 is a current-decision ADR (one ADR per topic, from 0100), so this is an edit to it, not a superseding file. Add three things:

- **The carry.** A person's approval of the head the merge started from carries across any clean base merge: `build` runs again and `review` does not. The approval follows only the system's merges.
- **The conflict rule.** After a conflict, the review is scoped to the resolution, measured from the approved head, with no overlap paths kept open.
- **The known gap.** A restart in between loses the carry and falls back to #1274's rule.
