## Design note: #795, the footer sits at the bottom on a short page

**Where it goes: `apps/site/src/app/site.css`, and only there.** The site imports the board's stylesheet before its own (`apps/site/src/app/layout.tsx:5-6`: `import '../../../board/src/app/globals.css'` then `import './site.css'`). The `body` that has `margin: 0` and the background is the board's (`apps/board/src/app/globals.css:81-87`). Do not put the new rule in `globals.css`, even though that is where `body` is first styled. The board has its own full-height layouts that do not depend on `body` being a column (`.detail { display: flex; flex-direction: column; min-height: 100vh; }`, `globals.css:816-820`, and the lanes' `min-height: calc(100vh - 41px)`, `:266`). Changing the board's `body` is a board change, and this ticket is not one.

Extend the existing `body` rule at `site.css:52` (`body { font-size: 15px; line-height: 1.6; }`) or put a new rule right after it. Write `min-height: 100vh;` and then `min-height: 100svh;`. Use `main { flex: 1 0 auto; }` with a bare element selector, because `main` has no other rule in `site.css` or `home.css` yet. `.home` (`home.css:3`, `overflow: clip`) goes on the same element and does not conflict.

**The comment beside the rule** carries the ticket's cost-to-later-tickets line: every page is a fragment of `<Bar />`, `<main>`, `<Foot />`, so those three are `body`'s direct children (`app/page.tsx:11-13,251`, `app/docs/page.tsx:31-33,118`, `app/docs/[...slug]/page.tsx:97-99,130`, `app/docs/events/page.tsx:20-22,34`). A new page that wraps them in a `div` loses this layout without any error.

**One change that is not obvious.** A flex item starts its own block formatting context. So a top margin on `main`'s first child, or a bottom margin on its last child, no longer collapses through `main`: it now sits inside `main` instead of between `main` and its neighbours. `main` has no background or border, so the spacing should look the same. When you do the manual check, also look at the gap under the bar and above the footer on a doc page, not only where the footer ends up.

**Sticky elements.** `.doctree` and `.contents` (`site.css:309,335`) and `.ev-drawing` (`site.css:589`) stick relative to the viewport, and that does not change, because nothing between them and the viewport gets `overflow` set. Do not add `overflow` to `body` or `main` while doing this, or they stop sticking. Their `max-height: calc(100vh - 40px)` is not part of this ticket.

**Checks.** No unit test can see layout. `pnpm fmt:check`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` only show that nothing else broke. The behaviour itself is the ticket's "(a person)" checkbox.
