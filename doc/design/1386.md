### *Set the limits*: what the chapter must get right

- **The ceiling is `limits.day_usd` in `~/.lingtai/config.yml`, not the recipe.** `config.example.yml:104` has it commented out, as `# day_usd: 50`. That is an example value, not a default, so do not write that the default is $50. The comment above it (`:95-103`) says three things the chapter needs:
  - The ceiling counts reported cost plus rate-card estimates.
  - A run nothing could price is *"unknown and outside the figure"*.
  - `runtime.concurrency` above 1 needs a ceiling.
- **What happens at the ceiling, and lifting it.** Link `cli.md#lingtai-ceiling` for the command, and do not restate its timing. `cli.md:666` (*"at any time of the day"*) and `operating.md:1089` (*"refused until the ceiling has been reached"*) disagree. Resolving that is not this ticket's job, and a chapter that restates either one picks a side. If you want it filed, it is a backlog note.
- **Where you see the spend.** Use the footer on the log-reading pages (two figures, reported and estimated, *"never one sum"*), the bar turning to the fail colour past 80 % (#907, `doc/design/the-bar.md`), the banner when the ceiling locks work (ADR 0126), and `/spend`, one click from the footer. Take these from CLAUDE.md *Running Lingtai on Lingtai* and `the-bar.md`, and cite nothing from memory.
- **Keep the `error_max_turns` sentence word for word** (`guide.md:119`), because `explain.ts:279` cites it.
- **Per-run and per-ticket bounds.** `runtime.limits.{run,attempt,ticket}` and `evidence` are recipe keys, so link `reference.md#what-an-attempt-may-spend--runtimelimits-the-recipes` (rule a). Do not list each key's meaning.
