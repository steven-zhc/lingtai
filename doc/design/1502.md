## The search is wider than the guard. Judge what it finds against the code

`apps/board/unit/no-old-url.test.ts:15-26` (`OLD`) is the #1501 guard for code. It is too narrow for prose. It wants a quote or a host before `/pr/` and `/recipe/` (`START`, `:11`), and prose writes a bare `` `?project=` ``, so it misses `doc/operating.md:2032` and `doc/design/the-bar.md:13`. Search `doc/` with a looser pattern, leaving out `decisions-archive/` and `experiments/`:

    /task/|[?&]project=|(^|[^a-z@.-])/(pr|recipe)/

Then drop every `packages/recipe/` hit. This is a doc search, not a test. Do not extend `no-old-url.test.ts` to `doc/`, because that would put #1501's cost (*"any later comment that writes `/task/` fails the suite"*, `doc/design/1501.md:101`) on every design note.

What a hit maps to comes from `apps/board/src/lib/paths.ts`, not from the ticket's summary of it. Four of the mappings are easy to get wrong:

- **`/task/<id>` becomes `/p/<project>/ticket/<n>`** (`paths.ts:56`). Its `…/ticket` sub-tab is now **`…/history`** (`:62`). `/attempt/<k>` and `/sandbox` keep their names (`:65,69`).
- **`/?project=x` becomes `/p/x/ticket`, not `/p/x`** (`homePath`, `paths.ts:80-81`).
- **`/landed?project=x` becomes `/p/x/ticket/landed`** (`landedHref`, `:99`). **`/backlog?project=x` becomes `/p/x/backlog`** (`backlogPath`, `:90-91`).
- **`/pr/<p>[/<n>]` becomes `/p/<p>/pr[/<n>]`, and `/recipe/<p>` becomes `/p/<p>/recipe`** (`:84-87`). An anchor stays, as in `#sandbox` (`bar.tsx:114`: `` `${recipePath(project)}#sandbox` ``).

Some hits are **not** old board URLs and stay as they are:

- **`/report?project=`.** `/report` still reads it (`apps/board/src/lib/report.ts:130`, `project: first(params.project) || null`). `doc/operating.md:2032` is the `/report` filter list (section *Handled for you*, `:2004-2007`), so it stays. This is the same trap as the ticket's `recipe.yml`, but for `?project=`.
- **`/setup/repository/added?project=`.** `doc/design/1501.md:86`: *"It is a setup page, not an old board URL."*
- **Route-folder paths such as `app/task/[id]/page.tsx`.** These are source files from before #1498 moved them (`1498.md:8-11`), not addresses. They stay wherever they are cited as a line of code.
