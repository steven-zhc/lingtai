When it fails

Pick a failure. See where it goes.

Most are handled by a rule, for free. Some get one quick look. Few ever reach you. Every path has a limit, so nothing loops forever.

Counts: Lingtai's own log, this month PLACEHOLDER

Every path, as a list
  • The API went down at 3amhandled 41× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    It saved the half-done work, waited a minute, and redid the step from there. You never heard about it.

    At most 3 tries, after 1, 5, then 15 minutes. Then it asks you.

    Still down after the third wait? It stops, says which limit ran out and how many times it tried, and waits for you. The saved work is still there.

  • A test is flakyhandled 23× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Your checks went red. It ran them again on the same code, at once: green. It carried on, and noted the flaky test for you to look at later.

    One rerun only. Red twice means it is real, so the agent fixes it.

    Red twice: the agent fixes the code on top of its own work, up to 6 times. Still red: one closer look, then it asks you. If the approach looks wrong, it starts over from main instead, at most twice.

  • The reviewer found a bughandled 58× this month · asked a person 1×One look

    One quick look named the lines to change. The agent fixed them on top of its own work, and your checks and the review ran again.

    Up to 6 fixes. Then a fresh start, at most twice, or it asks you.

    Six fixes and still refused? One closer look. If the approach looks wrong, it starts over from main with a note on why, at most twice. Otherwise it stops and asks you.

  • The ticket is unclearhandled 9× this month · asked a person 9×You

    It stopped before guessing and asked you. Your answer went into the ticket, and it carried on from where it paused.

    Up to 5 stops per ticket. Then it waits for you to decide.

    After 5 stops on one ticket it stops asking. You edit the ticket, close it, or give it another go. The agent never answers for you.

  • The ticket is too bighandled 4× this month · asked a person 1×One look

    It ran out of time. One quick look split it into 3 tickets that run in order. When all three landed, your ticket checked its own list, line by line, and closed.

    Split once per ticket, into at most 8. A part that is still too big comes to you.

    A part ran out of time too. It is not split again: it stops and asks you how to cut it. The parts already landed stay landed.

  • Someone else changed mainhandled 112× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Someone merged into main. It merged the new main into its work and ran your checks again, then landed.

    Up to 10 merges per try. Then it asks you.

    Main moved 10 times under one ticket. That is rare; it stops and asks you whether to try again.

  • My Claude limit ran outhandled 6× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Nothing could start, so nothing was spent. It waited for your limit to reset, then carried on from the same point.

    Nothing to count: waiting is free. A limit with no reset, like a monthly cap, waits for you.

If the quick look itself fails, it asks once more, then hands it to you and says so.
