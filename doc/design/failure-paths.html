When it fails

Pick a failure. See where it goes.

Most are handled by a rule, for free. Some get one quick look. Few ever reach you. Every path has a limit, so nothing loops forever.

Pick a failure above. A rule tries first; if no rule fits, triage takes one look; you only hear about what needs you.

Every path, as a list
  • The API went down at 3amhandled 41× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Redone from the last commit after 1 minute. You never heard about it.

    At most 3 times, after 1, 5, then 15 minutes. Then it asks you.

    Still down after the third wait? It stops, says which limit ran out and how many times it tried, and waits for you. Every saved commit is still there.

  • A test is flakyhandled 23× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Your checks ran again on the same commit, straight away. Red, then green: it carries on, and lists the flaky test for you to look at later.

    One rerun only. Red twice means the code is wrong, so the agent fixes it.

    Red twice: the agent gets the real output and fixes the code, up to 6 times. If the approach looks wrong, it starts over from main with a note on why, at most twice. Then it asks you.

  • The reviewer found a bughandled 58× this month · asked a person 1×One look

    One quick look at the findings named the lines to change. The agent fixed them on top of its own work, and your checks and the review ran again.

    Up to 6 fixes. Then a fresh start, at most twice. Then it asks you.

    Six fixes and still refused? If the approach looks wrong, it starts over from main with a note on what went wrong, at most twice. Otherwise it stops and asks you.

  • The ticket is too bighandled 4× this month · asked a person 1×One look

    The agent ran out of time. Lingtai split the ticket into smaller ones, in order. Yours waits for them, then checks its own list was really done.

    Split once per ticket, into at most 8. Too big again? It asks you.

    A ticket is split only once. If a part is still too big, or your ticket comes back with work left that is too big, it stops and asks you.

  • The ticket is unclearhandled 9× this month · asked a person 9×You

    The agent stopped before guessing and asked you. Your answers went into the ticket, and it carried on from where it stopped.

    Up to 5 stops per ticket. Then it waits for you to decide.

    After 5 stops on one ticket it stops asking. You edit the ticket, close it, or give it another go. The agent never answers for you.

  • Someone else changed mainhandled 112× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    It merged the new main into its work and ran your checks again. Had the new main touched the same files, the review would have run again too.

    Up to 10 merges per try. A real conflict goes back to the agent, up to 6 times.

    Main moving 10 times under one ticket is rare. If it does, or a conflict needs a decision only you can make, it asks you.

  • My Claude limit ran outhandled 6× this month · asked a person 0×Rule

    Nothing started, so nothing was spent. It paused that account until your limit reset, then picked up from the last saved commit.

    Nothing to retry, so nothing to count. It waits, and the board shows when it resumes.

If triage itself fails, it asks once more, then hands it to you and says so.
