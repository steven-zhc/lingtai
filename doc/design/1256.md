## Decision 1: two copies of the cause, and the old one does not change

`TriageAdvised` is checked by the same `TriageResult.parse` that `decodeRow` runs on every row (`event-store.ts:257`). So any change to a sub-schema it shares with the new shape is a change to how history parses. The old shape has to be built only from frozen copies.

**Leave these exactly as they are, used only by the old shape:**
- `SplitProposal` and `SplitProposalAnswer` (`split.ts:174-210`). Their children are plain `z.object` with no `covers`. Adding `covers` to them as a required field would make every recorded `TriageAdvised` split fail to parse.
- `Hunk` (`domain/src/triage.ts:89`, which is `Mechanical | Divergent`).
- `LineFix` and `TriageQuestion`.

**Rename, then keep frozen, internal to `wait.ts`:**
- The current `causesWith(SplitProposal)` becomes `AdvisedCause`, with its six variants including `split?:`.
- The `TriageAdvice` enum stays as an unexported `const`.
- `adviceResolution` and `ADVICE_RESOLUTION` (`wait.ts:298-309`) stay exported, because readers turn an old advice into a button through them. Their type takes the advised enum.
- The `_triageAdviceCoversResolution` assertion (`wait.ts:315`) goes. A frozen enum is not meant to cover new resolutions.

**New:**
- `split.ts` gets `TriagePlan`, the recorded plan. Its children are `{key, title, body, kind, covers: string[]}`, and the plan is `{children, blockedBy, uncovered: string[]}`, with the same `refuseProblems`.
- `split.ts` also gets `TriagePlanAnswer`: the same, but children carry no `kind`. This keeps #1271's split, where the model never names a kind.
- `domain/src/triage.ts` gets `ContradictingHunk{_tag: 'Contradicting', path, line, why}`. It also gets a second union, `CauseHunk = Mechanical | Contradicting | Divergent`, used only by the new `ConflictingHunks`. The old `Hunk` keeps two members.
- `Cause` is built twice: `causesWith(TriagePlan)` is what gets recorded, and `causesWith(TriagePlanAnswer)` is what the model answers and what `TriageEnvelope` and `TRIAGE_ANSWER_JSON_SCHEMA` are generated from. Keep `ConflictingHunks`'s `.refine` (`wait.ts:900`) on the new variant.

The person's `Split` resolution keeps `SplitResolution`. The board's split editor (`apps/board/src/app/split-plan.tsx:32` types its prop off `RootCause`'s `split`) takes either plan shape and drops `covers` and `uncovered` when it prefills. Writing those into a body is #1261.

## Decision 2: `recommendedResolution` does not call `actionFor`

There are two reasons.

1. **It would create a load-time import cycle.** `routing.ts:9-11` says it: *"`attempt-next.ts` imports `wait.ts`"*. `respond.ts` value-imports `routing.ts` (`respond.ts:62`), so `wait.ts` value-importing `respond.ts` closes a loop at load time.
2. **Item 4's mapping is not `actionFor`'s output.** It maps to person resolutions, has no counters, and has no origin. For example, `TicketTooBig` without a plan from `diff-too-large` is `askPerson(limit)` in 0108 §6, but item 4 says `RedoStep`.

So write a small exhaustive switch in `wait.ts`, `causeResolution(cause): Resolution['_tag']`. It sits beside `recommendedResolution` with no `default`, and its doc comment points at 0108 §6. `UndecidedQuestion` maps to `EditBodyAndStartNewAttempt`, the resolution `resolve-wait.ts:433` writes `triageAnswersText` for.

The `Done` arm then reads:
- `TriageAnswered`: `causeResolution`, then drawn only if it is in `allowed`, otherwise `null`.
- `TriageAdvised`: `adviceResolution(resolution)`, then drawn only if it is in `allowed`.
- `TriageFailed`: the default.

All three special cases go, including #652's swap (`wait.ts:795-807`). The old arm loses them too: the ticket says an old card reads "as it did", but the swap and the drops are rules about two disagreeing fields. Keep them on the `TriageAdvised` arm alone, so old cards are pinned exactly as they render today, and let the existing tests for them stand.

`respond.ts` imports `Cause` as a type only (`import type`).

## Decision 3: `actionFor` takes its bounds as input and has no caller yet

No value for `ticketSplits` exists anywhere. `BOUNDS` (`respond.ts:578-589`) has `stepRedos` and `personAsks`, `RoutingPolicy` (`routing.ts:36-43`) has `refusalFixes`, `newAttempts` and `conflictResolutions`, and `Spent` (`attempt-next.ts:149-158`) spends under different names (`rounds`, `conflictRounds`).

So the signature is:

```ts
actionFor(cause, origin: 'run-limit' | 'diff-too-large' | 'other', bounds: Record<BoundName, {used: number; allowed: number}>)
```

- `BoundName` covers `refusalFixes | newAttempts | ticketSplits | stepRedos | conflictResolutions | personAsks`.
- `askPerson(limit)` carries `{bound, used}` straight from that record.
- Nothing in `src/` calls it in this ticket. #1258 wires it and decides where `ticketSplits` comes from. Do not add a recipe knob or a `BOUNDS` entry for it here.

How the bounds map in 0108 §6's `ConflictingHunks` row ("that action's bound"):
- `startNewAttempt` uses `newAttempts`.
- `resolveConflict` uses `conflictResolutions`, the same bound `offerFor` spends at `respond.ts:704-706`.
- `askPerson(unclear)` uses `personAsks`, as `UndecidedQuestion` does.

The order is fixed: any `Divergent` hunk first, then any `Contradicting`, then everything else. `hunks: []` (more hunks than are listed one by one) resolves to `resolveConflict`, and the mechanical answer relies on that.

## Decision 4: no `SCHEMA_VER` bump for `WaitTriaged`

The new member is additive. Old rows at version 2 parse unchanged through the existing 1→2 upcaster (`upcast.ts:179-192`), which keeps working because `TriageAdvised` still has `resolution`. A bump would need a 2→3 step in `upcast.ts`, and the ticket's *no upcast* is easiest to keep by not opening that file.

The cost: a process started before the merge cannot read a `TriageAnswered` row and refuses it at decode. Triage runs inside the daemon (`apps/cli/src/daemon.ts:76` imports `./triage.ts`), so only a *new* daemon writes one. What is left is an old board or CLI reading after a new daemon wrote, and `lingtai restart` plus a reload covers that as it does for every payload addition.

## The parser and the brief

- `parseTriage` stops building `{...found, _tag: 'TriageAdvised'}` (`conductor/src/triage.ts:432`). It parses the answer schema, then attaches the kind and builds `TriageAnswered`.
- A `resolution` key is refused by name before the schema runs, with a message saying triage answers a cause and the action is the system's (0118 §1). Then delete the `AnswerQuestion` and object-resolution branches at `:408-417`.
- The candidate filter at `:386` changes from `'cause' in v || 'resolution' in v` to `'cause' in v`. An answer with only `resolution` then falls through to "no JSON object". The resolution-first refusal reaches only objects that carry `cause`.
- Kind injection (`:419-430`) moves from `cause.split` to `cause.plan`.
- `TriageReply`'s `'advised'` becomes `'answered'`, with `Extract<TriageResult, {_tag: 'TriageAnswered'}>`.
- The brief: the `ANSWER_SHAPE` example (`:91-94`) uses a cause that needs no resolution. The cause list grows to seven.
- `RULES`: drop the bullets at `:149-151`, `:156-157`, and the `Split`/`Requeue` advice in `:158-162`. Keep the `DiffTooLarge` → `TicketTooBig` bullet, rewritten to say when to give a `plan` and when to give `spentOn`.
- The opening sentence (`:282-285`) loses "and recommend one resolution", which is part of the answer contract.
- The conflicts block (`:275`) loses "Propose a resolution" and names the three hunk tags.

## Readers

A cause matched by both shapes goes through two small domain helpers, so that no reader branches on the tag twice:

- `triageCauseOf(result): Cause | AdvisedCause | null`
- `triageAdviceOf(result): Resolution['_tag'] | null` (non-null only for `TriageAdvised`)

`domain/src/triage.ts`'s `triageAnswersText` and the asks it draws are typed on that union. They read only questions and `Divergent` hunks, and both shapes have the same ones. A `Contradicting` hunk asks nothing.

Board and status text for an old advice keeps its existing wording, and only `TriageAdvised` reaches it. `TriageAnswered` gets a line built from the cause alone.

Match on `WrongApproach.why`, `SetupBroken.what`/`repair` and `Unfinished.addToBody`, but only draw them. Nothing acts on them (#1258).

## Tests

- **No construction outside `wait.ts`**: a unit test reads `packages/*/src` and `apps/*/src` and fails on the text `_tag: 'TriageAdvised'` or `_tag: "TriageAdvised"` anywhere except `packages/domain/src/wait.ts`. It must match the construction form, not `=== 'TriageAdvised'`, which every reader still uses legitimately.
- **Old rows still read**: a fixture copied from today's `TriageAdvised` (including a `split` and a version-1 `ContinueConflict` row) goes through `parseStoredPayload` and then the `task_view` reducer. Assert the card's recommended resolution and the `status` line equal what they are today.
- **Rebuild check**: the ticket's *projection rebuild gives the same rows* is integration and cannot be verified inside a pass. The unit claim that stands in for it is the reducer test above: the reducer is pure, and a rebuild is that reducer replayed.
