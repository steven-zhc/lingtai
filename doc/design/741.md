Most of this change is laid out in the ticket. This note covers what the ticket leaves out: prose and assertions beyond the four places it names, and which parts of the existing test to keep.

**Two more comments describe the 22rem clip.**
- The `.segs` comment, `apps/board/src/app/globals.css:476-487`, says "wrapping at the width a column actually gives a card (22rem)" and "a word clipped to five characters (`.slab` below, and the number is computed there rather than claimed)". The ticket names only the `.slab` comment at `:553-574`. Rewrite this one as well. Keep its argument: label width is spent instead of a segment (0016 §4). Drop "22rem" as *the* card width and drop "five characters". The words are now cut "with an ellipsis where they must".
- `doc/design/the-card.md`'s *A label's tail* section runs from `:189` into the next paragraph, "**And the margin is three characters, not one.**". That paragraph is about whether the ten names stay distinct, and the ticket drops distinctness as a requirement. Delete the paragraph, or replace it with one sentence: at the narrowest lane two labels may read the same (`pr…`), and their position and the `title` tell them apart. The `globals.css` `.slab` comment makes the same claim ("Every one of the ten is still unique at that length, with **three** characters to spare"), so it goes too. `the-card.md:14` ("wrapping to five rows at `22rem`") is about the old chip row, not the rail, so leave it alone.

**The test: change one `it` in place and leave the other alone.** The block is `describe('what the column can give a card')` (`apps/board/unit/rail.test.tsx:1156`).
- Rename `it('keeps the ten names apart at the width 22rem gives them')` (`:1176`) to say what it now guarantees: whole characters at any width. Delete these:
  - the `fontSize`/`gap`/`padding`/`tracking` arithmetic
  - `shown`, `.toBe(5)`, the clipped-words list and the row
  - the `new Set(clipped).size` uniqueness check and the `collidesAt` margin check
  - the `text-overflow: clip` regex at `:1248`
- Keep the `grid-template-columns: repeat(10,` assertion at `:1241` and `white-space: nowrap` at `:1247`, because the ten-column grid is out of scope and must stay pinned. Add `overflow: hidden` and `text-overflow: ellipsis`, matched inside the `.slab { … }` block the same way the existing regexes do. Add a negative match for `clip` in that block too, so a later edit cannot quietly put it back.
- Rewrite the docstring at `:1157-1175` to match. Its point 2 ("every one of the ten is **still unique**") is the claim the ticket removes.
- *carries the whole name on every segment* (`:1252-1257`) already asserts `title="${step}:` for every step. That covers the ticket's "every segment's `title` starts with its step name", so don't write a second test for it. Only its doc line needs changing: "The `title` the clip leans on" becomes "the ellipsis".

**Don't add a pixel-width test in place of the old one.** No unit test can tell where the browser's ellipsis cuts: the board's unit tests read the CSS source and render to a string, and nothing lays the card out. The `.slab` declarations are the unit-level check. The manual resize in *Done when* is the only check on what actually renders. The integration half (Next build) doesn't cover it either, so say this in the PR rather than suggesting a check exists.

**`text-overflow: ellipsis` on its own is enough at the narrowest lane, and no ADR changes.** At `.col`'s `min-width: 200px` (`globals.css:268`) a segment column comes to about 15px. A glyph is about 5.1px (9px × 0.57, the test's own figure at `:1194`). That leaves room for the ellipsis plus one or two letters. Even narrower, the browser shows a bare `…`, never half a glyph. Don't add `min-width` or `max-width`, change the font, or add short labels. Those are out of scope. No ADR names the clip (0016 §4 and 0061 §5 concern segments, not labels), so no superseding file is needed.
