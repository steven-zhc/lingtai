## The rule, in order

1. **Paragraph.** Strip each line, skip the leading empty lines, take lines up to the next empty one, and join them with a single space. Scan only after joining, because a backtick span or a link can wrap across lines. If the result is empty, return `null`.
2. **Scan once, left to right, tracking two states:** inside a backtick span (toggled by `` ` ``), and inside a link. A link is `[` … `]` and, when `(` comes straight after the `]`, everything up to the matching `)`. A plain parenthesis, as in `(#608).`, protects nothing. Only a link's parentheses do, because `(#608).` has to end the sentence.
3. **A candidate end** is `.`, `?` or `!` outside both states. After it, consume any run of closers: `*`, `_`, `)`, `"`, `”`, `’`. What follows must be whitespace or the end of the paragraph. This closer step is the decision the ticket leaves implicit, and it is what makes `**bold.**` and `(see x.)` end correctly. The ticket's "followed by a space or the end", read literally, would never cut `**bold.**` at all. The summary includes the closers.
4. **A candidate is not an end** in either of these cases:
   - the word it closes is an abbreviation: a small fixed list of `e.g.`, `i.e.`, `etc.`, `cf.`, `vs.`. `events.ts:1238` has *"The branch, e.g. `main`."*, so `e.g.` is real here.
   - the next non-space character is a lowercase letter. This catches abbreviations the list misses.

   `0110 §9.2`, `events.ts` and `v1.2` never reach step 4, because there is no whitespace after their `.`. A `§9.` followed by a space and a capital *is* a sentence end. That is the reading I took of the ticket's "`0110 §9.` must not cut early": `see 0110 §9. Then more.` returns `see 0110 §9.`, not a cut inside `0110`.
5. **Unbalanced bold.** Count the `**` outside code spans in the chosen prefix. If the count is odd, extend to just after the next `**`, then stop there. The ticket's Edges ask for this. None of the twelve current comments needs it: `RunKeptUncommitted`, `PassRouted` and `ProjectRefused` all close their bold before the period. Test it with a literal.
6. **No end found:** return the whole joined paragraph. Add no ellipsis.
