## The decision the ticket leaves open: what "prose is shorter" measures

Today `proseOf(tutorial.md)` is **431 words** (code fences stripped, `docs.test.ts:121-125`). This change adds words that the ticket fixes or ADR 0112 §21 requires:

| Added | Words, about |
|---|---|
| The two legends, copied verbatim from `tutorial-board.md` on the branch | 145 |
| SVG alt text: §21 says it "equals the SVG's `aria-label` or `<desc>`" | 32 |
| The SVG's required line "Open the diagram at full size" (§21) | 6 |
| Two PNG alt texts, one sentence each | 25 |
| The writer/reviewer table and the recommendation | 45 |

That is about 250 words. If the whole `proseOf` total had to fall below 431, the running text would have to drop to about 180 words. That would cut the token paragraph that #1411 says stays, and Step 2.

**So the rule is this.** "Shorter than before" means the chapter's running text, which is `proseOf` with these removed:

- the three image lines
- the full-size line
- the two numbered legend lists

That running text must come in **well under 431; aim for 330 or less**. The whole-file `proseOf` must stay under 800 (the test), and should land around 600–650. Put both numbers in the commit body, so the reviewer can check the claim rather than guess at it.
