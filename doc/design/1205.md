# #1205: design note — continue an attempt that an account limit cut off

## Where the ticket's pointers have moved

The dependencies have landed (#1194, #1201, #1203, #1204, #1170), so some of the ticket's line numbers are out of date. Use these:

- **The wall matcher is `wallOf`, not `accountWallOf`.** It is in `packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:311-314` and calls `classify` from `failure/`. 0108 §11 names it `accountWallOf`, and that is wrong. Fix the name when you edit §11.
- **The crash ending.** The blocked branch starts at `conduct.ts:5707`. The wait is written by `holdTicketEffect` at `:5833`. The lock comes after it, at `:5847-5852`. The commits are published before either, at `:5451` (`publishWhatIsCommitted`).
- **The continuation.** It is computed at `conduct.ts:1277-1406`. The run id is adopted at `:1413-1416`, and the claim branches to `continueTicketEffect` at `:2827-2831`. The re-cut `at` is at `:3026-3031`, the carry of kept work at `:3037-3087`, and `from` is built at `:5314-5333`.
- **The owner's transitions.** `continueTransition` is at `packages/domain/src/ticket.ts:855-880` and is what refuses anything not `approved`. `releaseTransition` is at `:888`. The release reduce is at `:562-579` and drops the cause, at `:572`.
- **The ticket reducer's version.** `ticketReducer.version` is `1` (`ticket-codec.ts:172`) and becomes `2`.

## 1. The ending: lock first, then release, and nothing in between

The new branch goes at the top of `if (outcome === 'blocked')`. That is after the publish, and before `waitReasonFor`, any `StepEnded`/signoff append and `holdTicketEffect`.

**When it applies.** All of these must hold:
- `pass.end.reason` is `AgentCrashed`;
- its `cause` is `'crash'`;
- its `step` is `design`, `implement` or `review`;
- `wallOf(detail)` is not null.

**What it does, in this order:**
1. **Lock.** Call `standDownAccount({ of: 'mid-run', step }, runtime, detail)`.
2. **Release.** Call `release(reason, { _tag: 'AccountLimit', runId, step, runtime })` through the owner's `releaseTicketEffect`.
3. **Return.** Return `ok: false, stage: step`.

No `WaitStarted` and no signoff is appended.

**The order matters, and the ticket's Edges say so.** If the release came before the lock, a look in the gap would claim the ticket and walk straight back into the wall.

**`standDownAccount` currently swallows a failed append.** It logs and returns `void` (`conduct.ts:1734-1736`). Change it to return whether the account is locked now: `appended` or `already` means yes, a failure means no.

**When the lock did not append, keep today's crash wait.** Releasing a ticket that nothing holds back means a loop of immediate walls, with nothing to bound it but the next look. This case is a read or append that failed, and it falls back to today's behaviour.

**What stays as it is:**
- The fixer's stand-down at `:5088-5091`. It answers `already` from now on.
- The existing `:5847-5852` lock, for every crash this branch does not take. That includes a judge's crash at `proposed` (`:3341-3343`), which is out of scope.

## 2. Where the continuation comes from: the claim's own read, not the look

**This departs from the ticket's *The look* edge, deliberately.** The ticket has the look hand `conductWork` a `continuing: { runId }` taken from the release.

**Why not the look:**
- **The look has no cause to read.** `selectRunnable` works on `task_view` rows, and `heldUntil` (`packages/conductor/src/queue.ts:148`) shows what such a row carries: no release cause. The look would need a ticket-stream read per row.
- **The claim already reads the right state.** `conduct.ts` already reads that stream through a snapshot at the version it will append at (`:1262`).

**So the look is unchanged, and `options.continuing` stays an approval's** (its doc, `conduct.ts:617-628`).

The `continuation` computation gets a second source beside `options.continuing`. The source applies when `reduced.lifecycle.status === 'backlog'` and `continuableRelease(reduced)` names a run.

**`continuableRelease(state)` goes in `ticket.ts` and is pure.** It looks at `state.claims.at(-1)`:
- It answers `{ runId, cause }` when that claim's ending is `released` and the cause's tag is in a `CONTINUABLE_CAUSES` set, which is `['AccountLimit']` today. #1211 adds `BaseRed` to the set.
- It answers `null` otherwise.

**The run id becomes `continuation.runId`, whichever source supplied it**, instead of `options.continuing.runId` (`:1413-1416`). The claim branch at `:2830` becomes `continuation !== null`.

**What this buys:**
- Every launch path (queue, detached slot, `lingtai now`) continues the attempt.
- There is no stale copy for the claim to distrust.

**The lock gate is already in front of every launch.** `apps/cli/src/conduct.ts:411` checks it for detached launches and `:456` for the others.

**The source still re-reads `acct-{runtime}`.** If the lock still stands, which is a race with a fresh lock, the source **declines at claim**. It never drops to an ordinary fresh claim. A release-sourced ticket must never silently become a new attempt from the base. That is exactly the cost this ticket removes.

**Backoff.** The ticket keeps its place, and `source.backoff` still applies from its claim's `lastAttemptAt` (`queue.ts:148-153`).
- At `until`, the backoff has normally passed, because `standDown` falls back to that same backoff (`conduct.ts:1720-1725`).
- After an early `lingtai account lift`, `lingtai now` takes the ticket at once (`backoffMs: 0`).
- `task_view` does not change.

## 3. The owner's continue transition

Extend `continueTransition` and leave its signature alone.

**Which state it accepts:**
- `approved`, as today.
- `backlog` whose `continuableRelease(state)?.runId === input.runId`.

**What it refuses:** anything else. `not-approved` keeps its meaning for the approval path. Add `{ reason: 'not-continuable', status }` for the backlog path.

**Two looks racing.** The append stays at `state.version` in `continueTicketEffect` (`packages/conductor/src/ticket/commands.ts:217-249`). The loser gets `ConcurrencyError`, then `lost-race`, then `notClaimed`.

**The reduce needs no change.** `TicketContinued` is already read as a continuation by its type (`claims.ts:22-23`). The claim entry keeps its `released` ending until the next ending overwrites it, because `endClaim` maps by `runId` (`ticket.ts:429-431`).

## 4. Which head, which `from`: one pure function per cause

Put `continuationOfRelease(cause, attempt: AttemptState, runEvents)` in `packages/domain`. It returns `{ cut, carry, from }` or `{ cannot: why }`, and is what #1211 extends. For `AccountLimit`, it decides the cut from `savedHeadOf` (`packages/domain/src/attempt.ts:740-759`):

| `savedHeadOf` | Cut at | Ref check | Carry |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Saved{head, ref}` | `head` | the issue branch (today's `at`) | the `kept` entry whose `sha === published.armSha` and `parentSha === head`, if one exists |
| `WipHead{recorded: true}` | that kept entry's `parentSha` | none | that entry |
| `NoSavedHead`, nothing committed | `diffBaseShaOf(runEvents)` | none | the kept entry whose `parentSha` is that base, if any |
| `BranchDiverged`, `WipHead{recorded: false}`, or `NoSavedHead` where a commit cannot be ruled out | — | — | `cannot` |

**The `Saved` row carries a WIP too.** `savedHeadOf` answers `Saved` and ignores a WIP arm when the clean branch carries the head (`attempt.ts:743-746`). That is right for a grant, but for this ticket it would drop the uncommitted edits that the cut-off implementer made on top of a committed head.

**Treat "nothing committed" strictly.** If the published row cannot show that no commit was made past the base, take the `cannot` path. That costs a restart, never a silently lost commit.

**`provisionWorktree` needs a cut with no ref check.** Today `at` always checks `atRef ?? branch` (`packages/repo/src/worktree.ts:220-232`). With no branch on origin, that check refuses as *stale* — and a run cut off before its first commit has no branch, which is exactly the done-when's case.
- Add an explicit option for this, e.g. `atRef: null`, meaning *a commit, nothing to have moved*.
- Never change the default.
- A sha the mirror does not hold fails `worktree add`. That counts as `cannot`.

**Give the stale refusal its own typed failure**, so the ending can tell it apart without parsing the sentence. Either give `RepoFailed` a field or add a tagged error.

**Carrying the WIP.** Extend the guard at `conduct.ts:3042`, which is `continuation === null && previous && keptWip`. For a release-sourced continuation, the kept entry comes from `continuation.carry`, not `lastRun`, and `RunAppliedKeptWork.fromRunId` is the run's own id. A carry that wedges stays `notCut`, as today.

## 5. When the head cannot be continued

This covers two cases: `cannot` from §4, and a cut refused as stale. Both happen **after** `TicketContinued`, because a release needs a claim to give up.

**Record it.** Carry a `headGone: string | null` flag out of the cut.

**Restart if one is left.** Check `headGone` before the restart branch at `conduct.ts:5677`. If `reduced.restarts.length < ceiling.restarts`, call `release(reason, { _tag: 'Restart', restart: n })`, as `:5682-5695` does.

**Otherwise, hold for a person with no new `WaitReason`.** Use the cut-off run's own `AgentCrashed` (step, `cause: 'crash'`), with its detail extended to name the missing head and its ref. Its resolutions already offer `Requeue` (`packages/domain/src/wait.ts:354-358`), which is 0108 §15's floor.

## 6. The rerun inside the walk

**`runAttempt`'s `from` gains a `rerun` field:**

```
rerun?: {
  for: { step; name; round };
  prompt: string | null;
  accountReruns: number;
}
```

**Set the first visit's context:**
- `rerun`: the dispatch suffix (rule below);
- `round: for.round`;
- a new `ActionContext.recorded?: string`.

**Strip `recorded` where `rerun` is stripped**, right after the cut-off action (`packages/actions/src/action.ts:1051-1054`).

**The actions before `for.name` in the step's list do not run again**, as with #1201's `resumedAfter` (`action.ts:949-967`). Build them from the cut-off visit's own `StepEnded.outcome.actions`.

**Recipes are read per pass, so the list may have changed.** If the step's list no longer has `for.name` at that position, visit the step afresh, as a first visit. Write a run-log line saying so.

### The suffix arithmetic (the non-obvious part)

`rerunId` appends `:rerun:<context.rerun>` (`action.ts:1292-1295`). A transient rerun currently passes `transientRetriesSpent` (`run-attempt.ts:1912-1913`).

**Reusing a counter collides.** Say an account rerun takes `:rerun:1` while the transient count is 0. If that run then meets a 503, the transient rerun also computes `:rerun:1` on the same base id. The runtime refuses the reused session id.

**Make the suffix `transientRetriesSpent + accountRerunsSpent` for both kinds**, computed after the kind's own counter goes up:
- Each rerun raises exactly one of the two counters, so the sum strictly increases and never repeats.
- With no account reruns (`A = 0`), #1201's ids are unchanged.
- The first account rerun on a clean attempt is `:rerun:1`, which is what the done-when asks for.

**Where `A` comes from: count it, don't add an event.** `A` is the number of `TicketReleased{AccountLimit}` naming this `runId` on the ticket stream. It is already there, so add no new event and do not bump `run-codec`.

**Give a release-sourced continuation its own key.**
- The judge and conflict dispatch ids use `continuation?.resumedWaitId ?? 'start'` (`conduct.ts:3252`, `:4894`).
- A release-sourced continuation must not reuse `'start'`: its `conflictFixes` restarts at 0, so `…:merge:start:1` would repeat an earlier pass's id.
- Give it a key per continuation, derived from `A`.

### Who reads `recorded`

There are four dispatch sites. Each uses `context.recorded` instead of composing a prompt, and still calls `prompted(...)` with it, so the rerun's own `RunPrompted` is on the log:
- the reviewer, `packages/actions/src/agent-action.ts:986-993`;
- the design agent, `agent-action.ts:1640-1650`;
- the implementer, around `conduct.ts:4567`;
- the fixer, in `fixRound` at `conduct.ts:4841`.

**A fixer is selected by `for.round > 0`.** Its prompt's content does not matter, because it is the recorded one. What `round` decides is the dispatch id, `${runId}:fix:${round}`, and the unhooked settings.

### Building the prompt

`prompt` is the last `RunPrompted` on the run stream whose `for` deep-equals the cut-off call, expanded with `expandPrompt` and `resolvePromptRefs`.

**`resolvePromptRefs` returns a `Promise`** (`packages/conductor/src/prompt-refs.ts:117-125`). Wrap it in `Effect.tryPromise`, so you add no `async` function. Do not raise the ratchet's baseline.

**The prompt is `null` in these cases:**
- a ref that does not resolve;
- a v1 record with `prompt: null`;
- a `reduced.pendingPrompt` set while the ticket was queued. The claim consumes that edit (`ticket.ts:525`), so handing the old prompt would drop it silently.

With `null`, the step's brief is built afresh and the run log says why.

## 7. Reduce and codec

- **The `released` ending carries the cause.** `AttemptEnding`'s `released` gains `cause: ReleaseCause | null` (`ticket.ts:38-40`). `TicketReleased` sets it at `:572`, and `ticket-codec.ts:30`'s strict shape gains it too.
- **Snapshots are voided.** `ticketReducer.version` goes from 1 to 2.
- **The new cause.** `ReleaseCause` gains `AccountLimit{ runId, step: 'design'|'implement'|'review', runtime: RuntimeId }` (`events.ts:283-288`).
  - Anything that switches over the four tags must handle it. Grep for `ClaimUnconfirmed`: today only `conduct.ts` and `events.ts` name it.
  - Regenerate `events.json`.
