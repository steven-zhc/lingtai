## The trap: a unit test reads a sentence in the gates section

`packages/conductor/unit/step-matrix.test.ts:1381-1394` reads `doc/guide.md` and runs this regex on it:

```
/(\w+) action kinds, of\s+which ([\s\S]*?)\n\(\[reference\]/
```

- The first word must be the numeral for `PLUGINS.length`.
- Every numeral word between `of which` and the next line that starts `([reference]` is summed, and the sum must equal the same total.

Today the sentence is at `:189-193`: *"Fourteen action kinds, of which four produce a verdict, two run for effect, one keeps…, one reads it back, and six are names…"*, with the `([reference](…))` link opening the next line.

When you rewrite the gates section:

- Keep that sentence as it is, and keep the newline before `([reference]`.
- Do not put any numeral word (`one`, `two`, `ten`…) inside its span.
- Do not write the phrase "action kinds, of which" anywhere earlier in the file, because the test takes the first match.

`pnpm test` is what catches a mistake here. Markdown is not run through `oxfmt`: `package.json:16-17` globs only `.ts`, `.tsx` and `doc/events.json`. So nothing will reflow the sentence for you, and nothing will reflow it behind your back either.
