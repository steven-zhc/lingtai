# Design note — #539

Make `GrantFixRound` a distinct wait resolution carrying `onSha`. Put eligibility in `allowedResolutions`, so the CLI, board and resolver use one rule. Offer it for a refusal with a usable fix criterion and preserved work, including fixable review findings, a red build and red verification; do not infer eligibility from triage’s recommendation. Keep `Approve` on its waiver path: `resolve-wait.ts:357-365` says “Approve absorbs the waiver,” while ADR 0108 §8 says a grant authorizes “exactly one additional `implement` round.” ADR 0118 §1 calls this resolution `ExtraRound`; the requested `GrantFixRound` name supersedes that accepted contract, so record the naming decision in a **new superseding ADR**, rather than editing 0118.

Resolve the grant under the existing per-ticket decision lock and current `waitId` check (`packages/conductor/src/resolve-wait.ts:68-82,179-215`; ADR 0118 §11: “A wait is resolved by its own id”). Read the source `runId` from `WaitStarted.reason.run`, and select the saved head and ref from that run’s `RunProposedCompletion.headSha` and `RunRefsPublished` fields, including `armSha` and `branchCarries` where WIP was saved. Those fields distinguish the branch head from an arm containing WIP (`packages/domain/src/events.ts:514-585`; its comment says the branch’s `headSha` is “never the arm’s” when WIP sits ahead). Require one identified, remotely reachable ref at the SHA being granted, and check that ref immediately before appending `WaitResolved{GrantFixRound,onSha}`. For a missing, unpushed or moved ref, leave the wait open and name the missing or changed ref and the recovery choice. The event and field that settle any particular held run are its latest `RunRefsPublished.outcome`, `headSha`, `branchCarries` and `armSha`; do not guess from the ticket or a local worktree.

Fold the grant into an owed continuation of the **same run**, and schedule it through the existing `ContinueRequested` path. That path already re-reads the ticket before reclaiming the run (`packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:938-950,2140-2151`; comment: “Resuming a held run, read fresh off the log”). Generalize its approved lifecycle and retry handling for the new resolution, without treating the grant as an approval or recording a waiver. Re-cut at the granted SHA with the worktree’s `at` guard; `packages/repo/src/worktree.ts:143-154` says a continuation names its own SHA and refuses a moved branch. Preserve the source run’s original diff base: `packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:980-991,1520-1528` warns that diffing against the re-cut head would show an empty change.

Enter `implement` with a fix brief made from the held refusal’s recorded evidence, and record the granted round as the next round on that run. The existing dispatch chooses `fixRound` only when `Brief.again` is present (`packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:3576-3605`), so a continuation that merely jumps to `implement` would wrongly invoke `firstDispatch`. Give the pass an explicit one-use granted-round input that supplies that brief and round number, then consume it on the first `implement` visit. Keep the recipe ceiling unchanged; subsequent routing must see the round as spent and cannot buy another from this grant. `packages/conductor/src/pass.ts:922-926,1143-1156` says `roundsSpent` carries across continuations and bounds the pass’s total; ADR 0108 §8 requires another human decision for another extra round. The normal fix dispatcher supplies the per-call limits and records spend (`packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:3395-3411,3460-3485`). Let the pass proceed through build, review, proposed and merge after that call.

Unit tests should pin eligibility, triage’s lack of authority, the event and ticket fold, ref selection and stale-head refusal, the original diff base, one fix call with its round and spend, a second grant after a new wait, and a refusing gate after the granted fix. Run `pnpm fmt:check`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck` and `HOME=/nonexistent pnpm test`. The 803-second integration suite cannot verify this before merge; the unit-level substitute is that the continuation re-enters the pass at `implement` and cannot reach landing without fresh gate visits.
