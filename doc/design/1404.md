# #1404: put everything the agent must follow before the long parts of the brief

## Where the text actually comes from (the ticket gets two places wrong)

- **The fix brief is built in two places, not one.** `fixBriefParts` (`packages/conductor/src/fix.ts:474-647`) returns the header through the outcome blocks. The call site then adds two more parts *after the diff* (`packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:5605-5606`): `## Also for this project` (the recipe's `spec.prompt`) and the redo paragraph. `## Also for this project` holds instructions, so it moves too, even though the ticket doesn't name it.
- **The implement brief is not built in `action.ts`.** It is the template `prompts/ticket.md`, filled in by `renderPromptParts` (`packages/conductor/src/prompt.ts:259-326`):
  - The template opens with `{{body}}` at line 5, and the outcome section is at lines 49-83.
  - `renderPromptParts` appends the design document after the template's last line, because `ticket.md` has no `{{design}}` slot (`prompt.ts:309-313`: *"a template with no slot gets it appended"*).
  - `conduct.ts:5193-5212` then appends three more things after everything: `alsoText(spec.prompt)`, `splitReturnParagraph` and the redo paragraph.
  - So today the implement brief puts a long body before its instructions and puts the design, the recipe's words and the redo paragraph after the outcome section.

## Shape

**1. `fixBriefParts` places every part itself.** Give it two new optional inputs: `redo?: string`, the text `redoNoteOf` returns, and `also?: string`, the recipe's prompt. The call site at `conduct.ts:5574-5607` passes them and appends nothing after the brief. Keep the result a `RecordPart[]` and reorder the parts, never the joined string. `recordPrompt` works out the `TicketBody` and `Diff` reference offsets from the parts (see the comment at `conduct.ts:5603-5604`, *"Appended as a part, never to the finished string, so no recorded offset depends on where the text before it happened to end"*), so moving a part moves its offset correctly without further work.

The order:

1. The two opening lines. One says the final message ends with an ```` ```outcome ```` block and names `## If this is not yours to fix, declare \`Disputed\``. The other says the diff is the last section.
2. The title line and the "not given the reasoning" paragraph.
3. `redo`.
4. The evidence, the open-findings context, then advice, declined and overruled.
5. The criterion, the change-nothing-else rules, commit, and `checksBrief`.
6. `also`, under its existing heading `## Also for this project`.
7. The Disputed / Implemented / Unclear section.
8. `## The ticket the reviewer held this change to`: title and body.
9. `## The change under review`: the diff, last.

**2. The implement brief.** Change three things:

- **Reorder `prompts/ticket.md`.** The two ending lines come first. Next come what is true, what to do, `{{checks}}` and the outcome section. After that, `{{failure}}`, then a new `{{design}}` slot, then `## #{{issue}} — {{title}}` and `{{body}}` last.
- **Fix the template's wording.** The line *"The ticket is above in full"* (`ticket.md:9-10`) becomes "below".
- **Prepend the code-added parts.** At `conduct.ts:5193-5212`, `alsoText`, `splitReturnParagraph` and the redo note go immediately after the template's opening lines, not after the template.
  - Do this with a small exported helper beside `redoParagraph` in `packages/actions/src/action.ts`. It splits a rendered parts list after its opening two lines.
  - The helper finds those lines by an exported constant, so the template and the code cannot drift apart.
  - If a project's custom template doesn't start with that constant, the parts are prepended at the very start instead. That keeps them out of whatever long text the custom template puts later.

**3. `withRedoParagraph` (`action.ts:1354-1361`) has to accept both layouts.** A brief recorded before this change has the redo paragraph at its end, and a brief recorded after it has the paragraph at the top. `recordedOr` (`action.ts:1341-1351`) and the two call sites that replay a recorded brief (`conduct.ts:5174`, `:5576`) pass it either kind. The function should:

- strip a trailing paragraph, using the existing regex, for old recordings;
- strip a paragraph that follows the opening lines, for new recordings;
- insert the new paragraph after the opening lines (or at the start, as above).

Design note 1246 (`doc/design/1246.md:71`) explains why a stale one must be stripped: *"or the agent is told two different began at / committed to pairs"*.

**4. The design brief moves with it.** `makeDraftAction` (`packages/actions/src/agent-action.ts:1722-1728`) shares `recordedOr` and `withRedoParagraph`. If its fresh path kept appending the paragraph while its replay path put it at the top, one step would word its redo two ways. So its fresh path also puts the paragraph first. It gets no ending lines, because it doesn't end with an outcome block. `agent-action.test.ts:2753`, which asserts `endsWith(redoParagraph(REDO))`, is updated to assert `startsWith`.

## Decisions the ticket doesn't make

- **One word of `redoParagraph` changes.** Its #1398 tail ends *"or another block as the brief above shows it"* (`action.ts:1381`). At the top of the brief, "above" points at nothing, so it becomes "below". Everything else in the text stays as it is, which is what the ticket's *"text is unchanged"* is protecting.
  - `withRedoParagraph`'s regex must still match "above" so old recordings get stripped.
  - `with-redo-paragraph.test.ts:33-35` pins the old wording and is updated in this change.
- **0108 §12 says where the paragraph goes, and this change contradicts it.** `doc/decisions/0108-a-refusal-buys-a-round.md:276` reads *"An agent's brief ends with one paragraph: this step was interrupted …"*.
  - This project's own instruction says a change that contradicts an accepted ADR should get a superseding file, not an edit. The ticket's Edges and `CLAUDE.md` both say otherwise: *"one ADR per topic, in `doc/decisions/` … A decision that changes is an edit to its topic's ADR"*. Only `doc/decisions-archive/` is append-only.
  - I followed the ticket: edit that one sentence of §12 to say the brief *opens* with the paragraph, after the lines naming how to end. If the operator prefers a superseding ADR for this, do that instead.
  - No ADR states the fix brief's section order, so none is added.
- **The design document counts as long.** It goes after the outcome section in the implement brief. It is read whole when the brief fits, and this ordering only decides what is lost when the brief doesn't fit.
