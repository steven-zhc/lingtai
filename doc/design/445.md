### 3. Paths and shas for `MergeRefused`

`integrate()` returns only `{reason, detail}`. The `IntegrationRefused` it appended carries `paths`, `baseSha` and `headSha` (`events.ts:1150-1161`). `domain/src/integration.ts:30-35`'s `Refusal` interface drops them from the type, even though the reducer stores the whole payload.

2. Add one helper that both `conduct.ts` and `approve.ts` call: read the integration stream, `reduceIntegration`, then `refusals.findLast(r => r.workItemId === … && r.branch === branch)`.

### 5. Three corrections to `legacyColumns` as attempt 2 left it

1. **The unpushed parenthetical is on the wrong field.** Today `blocked()` appends it to `diagnosis.what` and leaves `question` alone (`conduct.ts:3184-3193`). `legacy-columns.ts` appends it to `question`, and so to the card, the GitHub comment and the notification. Move it onto `what` in every arm.
2. **Compose the step prefix from `step`, and do not store it in the log.** The ticket wants *"a person's words in the variant that owns them"*, so each variant carries the bare words. `legacyColumns` rebuilds today's card line:
   - for `Asked`, `Human`, `CheckRed`, `DidNotFinish` and `SetupRefused`: `` `the \`${step}\` step ${word}: ${text}` ``, where `word` is `asked`, `held`, `refused` or `did-not-finish`, and `text` is `question` for `Asked`/`Human` and `why` for the others;
   - `raw` is the bare text, or `hold.raw`, which is what `diagnosis.raw` holds today.
   
   `Asked.question` and `Human.question` are stored unsaid, so the line differs from today only past 400 characters or where the text has runs of whitespace. Make the Done-when examples short, and say this in a comment.
3. **`MergeRefused`'s `question`** is `` `${reason}: ${raw}`.slice(0,400) `` today, against `said(why,400)`. Store `raw` unsaid and accept the same two-character edge, or apply the same collapse in `legacyColumns`. Do not move `said` into domain for this.

The losses already documented in `legacy-columns.ts`'s header stay accepted: the `<branch> is at <sha> and` prefix, `N of M rounds`, and the ceiling's limit number. Also accepted: `CheckRed{step:'merge'}` loses the `${because}:` card prefix it had through the merge branch.
