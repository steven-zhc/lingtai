## Design note — #537

Add a pure after-move fold in `apps/board/src/lib/task.ts` and pass its result through `TaskDetail` to `Standing`. Read the ticket’s own events in order: pair a `WaitResolved.waitId` with its `WaitStarted`, and use `WaitResolved.resolution`, `.by` and `.note` for the person’s move; use `TicketClosed.reason` and `.by` for the terminal close. Keep these facts separate from the current lifecycle. Today `standingOf` reduces the stream to that lifecycle, and its default `whoWaits` branch supplies “waiting for a conductor to take it” even for `closed` (`apps/board/src/lib/task.ts:725-760`, `apps/board/src/lib/task.ts:697-715`). The event contract says “which move a person made is a value rather than re-derived from which other event followed” (`packages/domain/src/events.ts:300-313`); the `TicketClosed` fold records the close reason (`packages/domain/src/ticket.ts:403-405`). The fold should show the latest completed move relevant to the present state, without carrying an old resolution forward as an available move after a later wait starts.

Render that result beside the state near the top of `Standing`: move, actor, recorded note or close reason, and only the subsequent outcome the events establish. Branch the terminal wording explicitly so `closed` cannot fall through to the queue hint or to a recommendation from an earlier diagnosis. Keep resolution controls conditional on a *current blocked wait*; the present renderer already tests `standing.state === 'blocked'` for their visibility (`apps/board/src/app/standing.tsx:670-688`). For `EditBodyAndRequeue`, distinguish `WaitResolved{EditBodyAndRequeue}` from a body write that succeeded while resolution was refused, and from a failed body write. The write happens before resolution, and failure can leave the wait unresolved (`packages/conductor/src/edit-body.ts:8-11`, `packages/conductor/src/edit-body.ts:205-232`). `IssueUpdated` and `IssueUpdateFailed` identify `change: 'body'` but carry no `waitId` or section key (`packages/domain/src/events.ts:1419-1441`), so do not attribute a nearby body I/O event to this move unless the recorded facts establish that link. A GitHub closed state establishes neither a body write nor a requeue.

Give completed and partial outcomes their own compact treatment, drawing the success, amber and red cues from `doc/decisions/0118/More.dc.html:204-236` without copying its unrecorded claims about queue position or daemon timing. Extend the ticket and progress reading surfaces to a resolved-page class in `globals.css`; today their stronger styling is scoped to `.detail--waiting` (`apps/board/src/app/globals.css:1136-1196`), and `TaskBody` applies that class only to `blocked` (`apps/board/src/app/task/[id]/page.tsx:761`). Preserve the ticket → state/progress → record order: the page design says an item that is not held has “no triage and no moves” (`doc/design/task-detail-page.md:12-33`). Unit fixtures should cover closed #524, recorded requeue, successful body edit and requeue, and each provable partial outcome at desktop and narrow widths. If the event fields cannot prove a step, assert that the page leaves that step unclaimed.
