## Where it goes

One file: the lane-header rules in `apps/board/src/app/globals.css:372-387`. Nothing in `page.tsx` changes.

## What sets the height: Landed's pill, not Waiting's count

The ticket's Fix says the `min-height` should fit "the tallest header, Waiting's 22px count". By the ticket's own measurements the tallest header content is Landed's pill:

- The pill is `.ctbtn` (`globals.css:378`): `padding: 3px 10px 4px`, a 1px border, and 11.5px text at normal line-height. That makes it 26.3px tall.
- Waiting's count is `font-size: 22px; line-height: 1` (`:387`), so 22px.

So the minimum has to clear 26.3px of content. That is also what the Watch-out row asks for ("a `min-height` it fits under").

## Padding counts inside the minimum

`* { box-sizing: border-box; }` (`globals.css:86`) means a `min-height` on `.col-h` includes the header's padding. Today Waiting overrides it to `padding: 1px 4px 11px` (`:386`) while the base rule has `0 3px 9px` (`:375`). With one `min-height` and two paddings, the two headers still come out the same height, but their content areas differ by 3px. So their labels sit 1.5px apart. Worse, if a later tall count overflows one of them, that header grows and the other doesn't.

The Fix row says the variants change type and colour "but not the header's box", so:

- **Drop the vertical part of Waiting's padding override at `:386`.** Keep `text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.08em`.
- The horizontal 4px can stay as `padding-inline` if you want to keep the label's left edge where it is today. It has no effect on the line being aligned.
- That moves Waiting's label up by about 1px. This is within "looks as before".

The rule then reads: `.col-h` gets `min-height: 36px` (the 26.3px pill rounded up to 27px of content, plus the 9px bottom padding). `align-items: center` is already on `.col-h` (`:373`), so shorter content centres with nothing added.

Write a comment beside the value saying that the Landed pill (`LandedCount`, `page.tsx:505-512`) is what sets it, and that Waiting's count is shorter. Otherwise the next person to shrink the pill won't know the number came from it.

## Why the minimum also fixes the streaming jump

The count's Suspense fallback is `<CountPending />` for every lane, Landed included (`page.tsx:1371`). `CountPending` (`page.tsx:817-821`) is a `.ct` holding only the 5px `.ct-pulse` (`globals.css:3633-3642`). So today, while counts load, Landed's header is about 26px and grows to 35.3px when the pill arrives: its cards jump by 9px.

The single minimum closes this with no per-state rule. The pending header, the zero count (`.ct.zero` changes only colour, `globals.css:3372`) and the arrived pill all sit inside the same 36px. Queued's fallback, `QueuedCountPending` (`queued-stream.tsx:482-488`), is a number plus a pulse, which is shorter still.

There is no need for a `.col.quiet .col-h` rule. The minimum lives on the shared rule.

## Don't move the "On their way in" header

`.col-h` is also used by the "On their way in" strip above the lanes (`pending.tsx:45`, `<header className="col-h">` inside `<section className="pending">`). That strip is not a lane and is not in the ticket. A 36px minimum there would add about 10px under its title.

Put the `min-height` on `.col > .col-h` (or `.col .col-h`, matching the `.col.hot .col-h` form at `:381`), not on bare `.col-h`. Leave the base `.col-h` rule as it is for `pending.tsx`.

## Narrow widths

`min-height`, not `height`, so a label that wraps at the 200px / 15rem minimum widths (`:368`, `:371`) grows its own header and isn't clipped. That's what the ticket accepts. Add no `white-space: nowrap`: that would trade the accepted wrap for horizontal overflow.

## Checks

There is no unit-level stand-in for this. It's CSS, and the ticket rules out a pixel test. The `getBoundingClientRect().top` check on `.col .cards` in a browser is the evidence, taken in three states:

- with cards in Running, Waiting and Landed;
- with Waiting at zero;
- while counts load (throttle the network, or reload and read during the Suspense fallback).

`pnpm typecheck` and `HOME=/nonexistent pnpm test` should be untouched by a CSS-only diff. `effect-ratchet.baseline.json` cannot move.
