## Design note for #447

Keep the catch-up in `apps/cli/src/triage.ts`, beside the live handler and its process-wide queue. Share the same queue between startup, recipe changes, and live `WaitStarted` delivery. Give a catch-up worker a final stream read before it spends money or records a second interruption; the live handler already re-reads before dispatch, and `enqueueTriage` holds the ticket-and-wait key until its worker settles (`apps/cli/src/triage.ts:39-55`, `:73-102`). For the second interruption, re-check the latest `TriageStarted` and absence of its `WaitTriaged` when appending; on a stream version conflict, read and decide again. The log’s `waitId`, `attempt`, and `by` identify the attempt to finish (`packages/domain/src/events.ts:1871-1902`). This keeps a person’s resolution or a concurrently completed triage from turning into another paid attempt.

Keep the last completed `triage:` hash per project in daemon memory. The pass needs to expose the resolved recipe for each project to its caller: `conductorPass` currently returns counts and refusals, while `conductWork` holds each project’s resolved recipe internally (`apps/cli/src/conduct.ts:79-88`, `:174-191`). Compare those recipes after the pass and catch up changed projects; record a hash only after that project’s catch-up succeeds, so a failed scan can be tried on the next pass. Use the same map for the catch-up after `loop.start()` (`apps/cli/src/lingtai.ts:990-1038`). The startup pass may have scanned first; the map and shared queue make the later startup call harmless. As the existing startup comment says, the subscription is already up then, so an arrival during the scan is not missed (`apps/cli/src/lingtai.ts:1030-1033`).

Treat the `waiting` card as a candidate, then let the ticket stream decide whether its wait is still current. `readTasks` retains open rows regardless of its two-day closed-item window (`packages/projector/src/task-view.ts:27-33`; `packages/projector/src/sqlite.ts:668-677`; `packages/projector/src/postgres.ts:305-315`). Let `triageStatus` decide eligibility from that current wait and its latest attempt; its comment specifies “`NotNeeded` first” for `OwnQuestion` and `SignoffRequired`, and its facts give `Done` precedence over recipe declaration (`packages/domain/src/wait.ts:656-691`). Thus removing `triage:` stops new work but does **not** turn a completed wait into `NotDeclared` or erase paid advice. That follows 0118 §1’s rule that “a recipe that stops declaring triage does not hide a result already paid for” (`doc/decisions/0118-a-hold-explains-itself.md:203-212`).
