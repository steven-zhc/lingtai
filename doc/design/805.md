Reported cost and the rate-card estimate both count. The header puts it this way: a null-`costUsd` row *"carries the estimate the `spend` fold stored … and is counted as an *estimate*; one nothing could price is *unknown*, and the ceiling cannot see it"* (`day-ceiling.ts:5-9`).
