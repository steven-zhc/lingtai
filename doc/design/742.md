# Design note: #742, the tutorial's refuse step and Part 1's figures

The change is `doc/tutorial.md` only. This note covers the word budget and where each sentence goes; the ticket already gives the wording.

## The budget, measured on this tree

I ran `docs.test.ts`'s own `proseOf` on today's file:

| Slice | Words now | Cap (`docs.test.ts:124-129`) |
|---|---|---|
| prose before `## Optional` | 939 | < 1100 |
| `## Optional` → `## Part 2` | 158 | < 350 |
| `## Part 2` → end | 347 | < 600 |
| whole page, code included | 1799 | < 2000 |

**The whole-page cap is the only one that binds.** `proseOf` strips fenced blocks, but `words` (`docs.test.ts:123`) counts everything, fences and table pipes included: each `|` is a token.

The new ticket text is 98 words and the old one is 56 (both `wc -w`). Pasting it costs **+42** before any prose is added, which leaves about 158 words for everything else. Spend them like this, so about 80 are left over:

1. **The fence at `:203-211`.** Keep the first two lines (`Add the next chapter to <a different story title>` and `labels: feature`), then the blank line. Replace everything after them with the Evidence block, verbatim, and keep its line breaks. Do not add words to it.

2. **The approve sentence**, about 25 words. Put it after "Then approve as in Step 4." (`:220`). Say that if the review still refuses, approving asks for a reason, and show `lingtai approve chapter-garden --issue <ticket-number> --note "<why>"` inline. Use `<ticket-number>` because `:193` uses it. Do not use the ticket's `<n>`.
   - The flag is real: `commands.ts:272` gives `lingtai approve <project> --issue <n> [--note <text>]`, and `:275` says "required when a…". `cli.ts:263` parses it. The ticket's `cli.ts:215` is the wrong line.
   - Leave the paragraph after the fence (`:213`, "so the first build is refused. Lingtai buys one fix round…") as it is. It is still true for the new text.

3. **Node.** Change "Node.js and npm" in the Chapter Garden row (`:13`) to "Node.js 26.13.0 and npm". That is two tokens and adds no row.

4. **Figures.** Write one sentence under the table, before "Everything else Lingtai needs…" (`:16`). Do not make it a table row: the columns are *For / Have ready / Why*, and a time does not belong under any of them. Roughly: *On 2026-10-29, with v0.10.0 and Claude Code, Part 1 took 13 minutes, 2 of them machine time, and cost $0.79; Codex reports no cost.*
   - Do not write $0, "free", or an estimate for Codex. B3's `~$0.00 est.` is not a cost.
   - The date and the binary stay in the sentence.
   - Do not add the refuse round's $1.61 or round B's figures. Each one costs words, and the ticket asks for neither.

5. **`--check-build`.** Add one row to the options table (`:90-100`), placed just above `--land hold` (after `--build ...`), since it is the build's companion: `` `--check-build yes` `` | Tries the install and build once during setup; Step 1 leaves it off. Keep the row to about 12 words.
   - Do not touch Step 1's command (`:61-65`, and the Fish copy beside it) or *Before you start*.
   - "Leaves it off" is accurate: `--defaults` skips the trial unless it is given `yes` (`commands.ts:78`, "try install and build once first").
