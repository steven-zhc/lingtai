## Why deleting it is "what marks a fix round", not a looser check

The ticket's *Watch out* asks for something that marks a fix or conflict round. Nothing on the visit says that directly, and the decision rests on this:

- `StepOutcome` carries only `round` (`packages/domain/src/action.ts:263-266`), never `conflictRound`.
- `StepReached` carries no context (`run-attempt.ts:576-590`).

So no field on `arrived` can tell a conflict round from a first visit. What can is `Disputed` itself. Only a fixer is shown findings to dispute, and the rule that enforces this is upstream:

- `decideImplementOutcome` turns a `Disputed` declared with nothing offered into `AgentFailed` (`packages/domain/src/implement-outcome.ts:122-124`): *"declared Disputed with no finding to dispute"*.
- The first `implement` call passes `findingsOffered: 0` (`packages/conductor/src/conduct.ts:5131`), and so does the abnormal-ending path (`conduct.ts:5070`).
- Only `fixRound` passes a non-zero count: `offered`, at `conduct.ts:5232-5237`. A conflict round counts as at least 1 there: `refusal.on === 'conflict' ? Math.max(1, refusal.findings.length)`.

So `disputedOf(arrived.outcome) !== null` (`packages/domain/src/attempt-next.ts:547-558`) already means "a fixer declared this". On a first visit it is null, as today.

Do not swap the clause for another counter. `conflictRoundsSpent` is not on the visit, and `roundsSpent` is the counter a conflict round does not advance (`run-attempt.ts:2143-2145`: *"A conflict round has its own counter and leaves `rounds` where it was (#653)"*).

Replace the `#1199, #1250` comment above the guard. It should say that the first visit is excluded by `decideImplementOutcome`, not by the round, and why the round cannot be used.
