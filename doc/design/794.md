# #794 — the events page, restyled to its design

## Read the design's cascade, not one block of it

`doc/design/events-page.html` holds three layouts plus a combined one, and the page #789 built is the **combined one, "A+C"** (`:168`). Its rules come in two layers, and the later one wins:

- **Spine**: start with A's `.spine` / `li::before` / `.node` / `.nm` / `.sp-out` (`:81-93`). Then apply A+C's overrides (`:169-177`). Those overrides change four things:
  - The spine sits on `background: var(--ground)` with `border-bottom: 1px solid var(--rule)`. It is not a surface box.
  - Each node is a `<button>` with `all: unset`.
  - `aria-pressed=true` fills the node.
  - `.filtering` dims the buttons that are not pressed (`opacity: .6`).
- **Entry**: start with C's `.entry` (`:157-166`), then apply A+C's `.entry` / `h3` / `.chips` / `.sum` / `.meta` (`:184-192`). Two of those rules carry the layout:
  - `.chips{margin-left:auto}` puts the step chips on the name's line, at the right.
  - `.meta{font:11.5px/1.55 var(--mono)}` with `.meta code{background:var(--surface-2)}` is the meta line, with the subscribe snippet inline.
- **Narrow**: at `:112`, below 760px, `.spine .nm{display:none}` hides the step names and `.sp-out` gets a smaller margin and padding.

Watch the names. In the design, `.ev` (`:61`) is the class for the **event name** (`font: 500 13px var(--mono)`). On the site, `.ev` is the **entry wrapper** (`events-body.tsx:53`, `site.css:604`). Keep the site's class names and translate the rules onto them. Do not rename to the design's classes:
- `.ev-chip[data-step]` is what the click delegation looks for (`events-filter.tsx:105`).
- `.ev` is the anchor target with `scroll-margin-top` (`site.css:604`).

Copy the design's values. Its tokens have the same names as the site's (`--surface-2`, `--accent-soft`, `--accent-ink`, `--rule-2`; see `site.css:241,531`). `site.css` does not define `--ground`. The sticky spine needs an opaque background, so use whatever token the body or doc layout is painted with. Do not use `--surface`, which is the box colour the ticket is removing.

## The commit note needs a second slot

`.source` cannot move by reordering markup inside `events-body.tsx`. `EventsFilter` renders `{children}` *before* `.ev-drawing` (`events-filter.tsx:109-110`), and the groups come after that, all inside one `<article>`.

Add a second prop to `EventsFilter`, for example `after?: ReactNode`, and render it after the groups' `map`, still inside `<article>`. `children` stays as the heading and lede.

This keeps all of it server-rendered, which matters: per `events-body.tsx:10-12`, *"Entries are rendered here, on the server, and handed to the filter as nodes; the filter only hides them."* The JS-off case stays as it is.

Do not take the heading out of the article to get around this. The Done-when lists the article's children in order.

## The spine's markup

Today the two side nodes are a separate `<ul class="ev-lane">` under the spine, separated by a dashed top border (`site.css:591`). In the design they sit beside the spine, in `.sp-out`, separated by a dashed **left** border.

Make `.ev-drawing` a flex row: the `ol` with `flex: 1`, then `ul.ev-lane` styled as `.sp-out`. Keep the `<ol>`/`<ul>` pair so the list semantics and the `role="navigation"` wrapper stay.

Change each button's content to match the design's `node()` (`:1198`):
- the count goes inside the circle;
- the step name goes under the circle;
- **drop the `i + 1` prefix** from the visible label (`events-filter.tsx:111`). The design shows the count and the name, not the step's position.

Keep `aria-pressed` and `disabled` (`:89-90`). `disabled` covers what the design does with `.zero`, so `.ev-node:disabled .node` takes the design's `.zero` colours as well as the reduced opacity.

Keep `.ev-clear` where it is. The design moves "show all" into a `.state` line, but that line is out of scope here.

Keep the existing `overflow-x: auto` and `min-width: 760px` for widths up to 1080px.

## The entry

- **Name**: replace `<code>{event.name}</code>` (`events-body.tsx:55`) with a `<span class="ev-name">`. The site's inline-code box applies to every `<code>`, and this name should not have one.
- **Chips**: wrap the step chips in one `<span class="ev-chips">` with `margin-left: auto`.
- **Snippet**: move it into `.ev-meta` as `· <code>…</code>` and delete the `<pre>` (`:83-85`). Give `.ev-meta code` the design's `surface-2` pill.
- **Width**: add `max-width: none` to `.ev p`. This is the actual cause of the 68ch width cap (`site.css:99`), and it is also why the chips wrap onto a second line.
- **Links**: add `.events a` with the same declarations as `.prose a` (`site.css:264-265`). Then add `.ev-meta a { white-space: nowrap }`, following the design's `.meta a` at `:189`.

## `label()`

Keep the basename and the line: `file.split('/').pop()` after splitting off the `#L…` fragment. The design's `label` at `:1170` does exactly that, so this matches the drawing. A URL with no line keeps its basename.
