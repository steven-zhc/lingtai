Mock · seven styles that are not a left rail. Two navigate (a dock, a palette), two stay on one board and put the other one beside it (a drawer, stacked boards), one joins them (one long pipeline), one starts from an overview (a hub), and one is the plain top bar. Each is drawn with Lingtai's content; the table at the end sets them against the rail.
