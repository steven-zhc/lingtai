# 0.12 — one set of books

**Status** shipped · 2026-11-06 · what 0.12.0 contains and what it left out,
as the operator agreed it. How each part is built is in its tickets, not here.

0.11 put the slogan on the front page: *Agents do the work. Lingtai keeps the
books.* 0.12 makes the books agree with themselves — one reduce per stream, so
the board and the conductor give one answer about one ticket — and makes them
easier to read: one bar, the spend in the report, and a queue that says what
waits and on what.

## What 0.12.0 contains

| Track | What is true | Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| **A. One reduce** | Every stream's reducer exists once, in `@lingtai/domain`; the board and task_view reduce with the same `apply`. | #797 and its children |
| **B. Effect 4** | The backend runs on Effect 4, and an attempt exported over OTLP is one connected trace. Turning the remaining IO into Effects continues in 0.13. | #790 (open; its rows move to 0.13) |
| **C. The receipt** | A landing's books are read on `/landed`, on the task page and in the comment a landing posts on the issue; #806 closed on that. | #806 (closed), #895, #896, #933 |
| **D. Setup** | Go, Cargo, Maven and Gradle repositories get suggested install and build commands. | #696, #697, #698 |
| **F. Navigation** | One bar on every page with Report at the right; a row per repository with its recipe; the Landed count as a button; a footer without links; the spend folded into `/report`, `/spend` gone. | #946 and #947–#953 |
| **G. The queue** | Queued shows blocked tickets greyed with their blockers, folds held tickets and idle epics into one line, and groups each epic's cards under a ring; the queue never takes an epic (a ticket with open sub-issues). | #957, #954, #960, #955, #956 |
| **H. The front door** | Real screenshots of the board in the site's hero (tabs, click to enlarge) and in a README cut down to a TL;DR. | #958, #959 |

## Not in 0.12

Each was raised in planning and set aside by the operator:

- **#704** (a setup refusal as two lines) and **E** (a page on integration tests after the merge): closed or dropped by the operator, 2026-11-06; to be reopened when a need is clear.
- **#790's remaining rows**: 0.13.
- The two receipt features #806 named and did not build — saying what is absent, and checking again — wait for a clear need.
- **A per-ticket spend ceiling** (#674): not urgent while the daily ceiling holds.
- **Pausing one project** (#675, #676): stopping the service is enough today.
- **Fewer review rounds** (#338): the data is collected, and the idea is not
  yet clear enough to build.
- **The daemon restarting itself when `main` moves**: it serves one operator,
  who restarts it.
- **Snapshots**, and #810 (the safety self-check).
